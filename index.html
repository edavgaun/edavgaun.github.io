  1. A real question. One sentence a manager would ask, not "I applied random forest to a dataset".
  2. The headline result first. A number with context, stated before the method.
  3. One chart that makes the point. Titled with the finding, not the variable names.
  4. An honest limitations section. What the analysis can't tell you and what you'd do next.
  5. Reproducible code. A linked script or notebook that regenerates every number on the page.
